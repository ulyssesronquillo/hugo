Akismet is a web service that detects spam. Akismet was originally created by Automattic, the company behind WordPress, to filter out spam comments on its own blogs. The use of Akismet has spread to other blogs and CMS engines. Akismet can also be implemented on other websites. There are a number of plugins and libraries available. Akismit also has an API. Akismet is open-source and free for personal use. Akismet offers professional and enterprise plans for a modest fee.
