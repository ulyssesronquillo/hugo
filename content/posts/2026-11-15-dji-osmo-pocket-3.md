I bought a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 back in mid September. I brought it with me on my Italy trip. I got some great footage of Sorrento, Capri, Positano and Amalfi, Rome and among other places. I came home and couple of weeks later, the screen would not stay in horizontal mode. I sent it to DJI for repair. Two weeks later they sent it back. I think they just gave me a replacement because the box was still unopened. Nevertheless, I'm back to taking videos. Today, I cut our banana tree in preparation for winter.  I got a [10 second time lapse](https://youtu.be/oxpzsU3oD18?si=pfTT103OHIDSlbrM) footage that might be worth seeing.
