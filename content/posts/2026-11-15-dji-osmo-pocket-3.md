I bought a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 back in mid September. I brought it with me to Italy for my trip. I got some great footage of Sorrento, Capri, Positano and Amalfi, Rome and among other places. I came home and couple of weeks later, the screen would not stay in horizontal mode. Some sort of mechanical issue. I sent it to DJI for repair in their Dallas facility. Two weeks later they sent it back. I think they just gave me a replacement because the box was still unopened. They sent back the original box. I now have two protective covers, 2 wrist straps, two short handles and 2 USB type C cables. Nevertheless, I'm back to taking videos. Today, I cut our banana tree in preparation for the winter.  I took a [10 second time lapse](https://youtu.be/oxpzsU3oD18?si=pfTT103OHIDSlbrM) footage that might be worth seeing.
