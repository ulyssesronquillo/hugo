I bought a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 back in mid September. I brought it with me on my Italy trip. I got some great footage of Sorrento, Capri, Positano and Amalfi, Rome and other places. I came home and couple of weeks later, the screen would not stay in horizontal mode. DJI repaired it. I think they just gave me a replacement because the box was unopened. Nevertheless, I'm back to business. I cut our banana tree today in preparation for winter.
