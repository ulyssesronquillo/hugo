If you have a video that you would like to share with others, how do you optimize it for the web. I’m using a simple open-source program called Handbrake which you can download for free. It’s available from [handbrake.fr](http://handbrake.fr). Handbrake is available on Windows, Mac and Linux. Based on my experience with working with Handbrake, I was able to shrink my videos to less than 10% of the original size without sacrificing quality.
