Just got done installing [Linux Mint](http://linuxmint.com) 19.1 Tessa Cinnamon edition. It’s a long-term support release with support until 2023. Here are some of the new [features](https://www.linuxmint.com/rel_tessa_cinnamon_whatsnew.php). I use Etcher on the Mac to write to the USB. I then booted via USB to install Linux Mint on the new machine. Linux Mint was done installing in less than 10 minutes. I went with Cinnamon since it was a darker theme.
