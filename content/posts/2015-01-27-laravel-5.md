It looks like Laravel 5 will be released sometime next week. [Laravel](http://laravel.com) is a PHP framework which uses simple RESTful routing, Eloquent ORM and Blade templating. It’s built on top of Symfony and Composer. It comes with PHPUnit for testing, and of course, it has migrations built in the package. If you’re curious about what Laravel 5 can do and some of its features, there are several [free videos](https://laracasts.com/series/whats-new-in-laravel-5) available at Laracasts about Laravel 5.
