Starting with iPhone 16, Apple introduced Fusion which combines sensor hardware with software processing to get better photographic results. The No Fusion camera app does the opposite. It minimizes the image processing and applies camera film simulations which Fuji users are accustomed to the last few years. The results are photos that look like they were not taken by an iPhone camera.
