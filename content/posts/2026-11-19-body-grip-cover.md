The body grip on my Fuji X-T30 ii lasted only a couple of years. After a trip to the tropics, it started to bubble up and come loose around the edges. Google search says that it happened to a few people in very hot conditions. The research was a disappointment knowing the camera wasn't built very well. I ordered the [body grip](https://www.ebay.com/itm/167229125219) from eBay hoping to fix the problem. It took about 3-4 weeks for the items to be delivered. Taking off the old body grip and putting on the new one was quite easy. The camera looks new. You can't even tell it has new grips. I'm not happy about the grips coming loose, but happy the replacement was a perfect fit.
