The body grip on my Fuji X-T30 II lasted only a couple of years. After a trip to the tropics, it bubbled up and come loose around the edges. 

Google search says that it happens to Fuji cameras in very hot conditions. It’s disconcerting to hear about the grips and the camera not built well.

I ordered the [body grip](https://www.ebay.com/itm/167229125219) from eBay hoping it willfix the problem. It took about 3-4 weeks for the item to arrive. Taking off the old body grip and putting on the new grips was quite easy. 

After the fix, the camera looks new. You can't even tell it has new grips. I was not thrilled that I had to replace the grips, but happy the replacement was a perfect fit, seamless and appears new.
