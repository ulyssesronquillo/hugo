Two months ago, I bought a [K&F Nikon to Fujifilm lens adapter](https://www.kentfaith.com/KF06.443_new-product-kf-m18115-nikon-nik-g-fx-pro-20-year-high-precision-lens-adapter-orange) that will allow Nikon lenses to be used on a Fuji camera.

I have several Nikon prime lenses for the picking.

- Nikon 24mm F2.8D
- Nikon 35mm F2D
- Nikon 50mm F1.4D

In addition, I also have a couple of Nikon G lenses.

- Nikon 24-85mm F3.5-4.5G
- Nikon 18-200mm F3.5-5.6G

It's not 100% compatible. It's all manual focus on the Fuji camera even though the lenses are autofocus.
