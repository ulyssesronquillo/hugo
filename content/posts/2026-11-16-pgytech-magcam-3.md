The PGYTech magcam camera grip integrates very well with the No Fusion camera app. Connect the device via Bluetooth and you can take pictures with the magcam camera button. You can press the button halfway to focus, use the zoom lever to zoom in and out. The magnet is strong so you never have to worry about dropping the phone. Works well if you like to take a lot of landscape pictures.
