Two weeks ago, my Ryobi leaf blower lost a fan blade. It was probably a small stick or some piece of rock that flew in into the blower and broke off the blade. I tried gluing the broken blade but to no avail. It's interesting that one little tiny blade can affect the operation of blower.

Instead of buying a new leaf blower, I found a replacement fan at eBay for $11 bucks. Removing the blade was a bit of challenge. Once you remove the cover and can get access to the motor, you have to hold the motor spindle with a pair of pliers and keep it stationary while rotating the blade assembly counter clockwise to unscrew it. Replace the blade assembly and screw it clockwise.

That was such a simple repair and it cost only a few bucks. Definitely worth it. Who knows it may run for another 10 years or so.
