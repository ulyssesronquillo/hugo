If you are tired of using the default Terminal prompt, then give [oh-my-posh](https://ohmyposh.dev) a try. 

Whether you're using bash in Linux or zsh on a Mac, oh-my-posh can style your prompt. 

Get familiar with oh-my-posh's syntax. It's not difficult once you get rolling. 

Browse several of the themes available on their [site](https://ohmyposh.dev) to get ideas. 

Head over to [Canva.com](https://www.canva.com/colors/color-palette-generator/) to get a color palette.
