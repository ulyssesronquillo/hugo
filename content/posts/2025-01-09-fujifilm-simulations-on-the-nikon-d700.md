You can add film simulation like features on the old Nikon D700. 

Just head over to [nikonpc.com](nikonpc.com) and download the presets. 

Follow the instructions tp activate on your camera.

Here are the simulations I've downloaded and tried.

```
B&W Contrast
B&W Portrait
Fuji Astia
Fuji Provia
Fuji SReala Skin
Fuji Velvia
Kodak Gold 100
```
