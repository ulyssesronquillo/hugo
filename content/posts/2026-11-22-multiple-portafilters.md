We have two espresso machines when we use to travel between two homes the last couple of years. We haven't been doing that lately. Both machines are now at our primary home. I usually get an early morning cappuccino and another one by mid morning. My wife drinks one cappuccino at a later time. 

We both drink single shots. I usually make a single shot early morning, then split the double shot together later on with my wife to save time. All this time I was swapping the single and double baskets. Then it dawned on me that I have an extra portafilter with the second espresso machine. 

So starting this morning, I'm now using two portafilters, one portafilter with a single basket and another with a double basket. No more swapping of baskets. It just works. So much easier to clean and maintain as well.

Espresso for the win.
