The other week, I bought the Ulanzi magnetic mount for the DJI Osmo Pocket 3. Now, I can attach my camera to any metal surface to capture your videos, whether it's the fridge, freezer, car hoods, railings, posts, etc to capture those camera angles. You can also use the device as a stand. The nice thing about it, is you still have access to your standard buttons, power, joystick and the rotating screen.
