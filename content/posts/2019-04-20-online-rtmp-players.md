Here are several online RTMP players:

- [Wowza Flash Player](https://www.wowza.com/resources/3.6.0/examples/LiveVideoStreaming/FlashRTMPPlayer/player.html)
- [Wowza Test Player](https://www.wowza.com/testplayers)
- [HLSTester](https://www.hlstester.com/)
- [HLSPlayer](https://www.hlsplayer.net/rtmp-player)

I prefer the Wowza test players. The last two has annoying ads.
