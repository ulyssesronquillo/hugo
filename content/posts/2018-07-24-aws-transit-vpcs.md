If you have multiple VPCs located in geographically diverse regions, you can connect them together using a new service called Transit VPCs. It’s a logically isolated VPC which can act as a bridge for launching and transferring data between regions. It can work as global transit center between other VPCs within your organization. Learn more by reading about it from AWS [announcement](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-solution-transit-vpc/).
