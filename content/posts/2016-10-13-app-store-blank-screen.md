The App Store on my iPad was displaying a white blank screen. I’m not sure what caused it.

It may have been my router. It stopped giving out dynamic IP addresses. For some reason it affected the App Store.

The only way I was able to fix it, was to go to my iPad’s **Settings** &gt; and select **iTunes &amp; App Store**.

1. Click on the Apple ID (email address) and then **Sign Out**.
2. **Reboot** the iPad.

Once the iPad has rebooted, the App Store started working again. Weird.
